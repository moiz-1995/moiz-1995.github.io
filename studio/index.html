Kids Studio

A private publishing tool by Agha Abdul Moiz

Kids Studio is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads finished videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, to YouTube channels that belong to its owner, and reads back those videos' public view counts.

It is not a public service. Nobody else signs in to it, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.

What it asks Google for

Permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (YouTube Data API). Nothing else.
